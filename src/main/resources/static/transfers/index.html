Send Money

Pay anyone by phone number, account number or UPI ID funded from your virtual wallets.

How it works

  • Phone numbers resolve against registered BMS profiles.
  • Pick the wallet that funds each transfer — e.g. pay for food from your Food wallet.
  • Recipient is credited instantly.
  • Every transfer is written to the audit ledger.

Quick Pay

Save payees once, then pay in two taps with preset amounts — ideal for repeat transfers.
