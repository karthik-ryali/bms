Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

Your privacy is fundamental to our banking architecture. This policy describes how your personal and financial information is handled within the Banking Management System (BMS).

1. Information We Collect

We collect user registration details (username, full name, email), account identifiers, virtual wallet configurations, and transaction records needed to maintain your account ledger.

2. How Your Data Is Protected

All passwords are encrypted using BCrypt salted hashing. All API communications require secure sessions or HTTP Basic authentication, and session tokens are stored securely in browser storage.

3. Transaction Data Integrity

Transaction ledgers are immutable and stored in a relational database with strict foreign key constraints and transactional rollback guarantees.
